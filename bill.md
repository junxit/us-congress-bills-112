# H.R. 2528

> To rescind the authority of the Secretary of the Treasury to develop a return-free tax system.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Taxpayer Freedom to File Protection Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Rescission of authority to develop return-free tax system

- **(a)** *In general* The Secretary of the Treasury may not develop or implement a return-free tax system.
- **(b)** *Repeal of prior authority* Section 2004 of the Internal Revenue Service Restructuring and Reform Act of 1998 is repealed.

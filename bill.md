# H.R. 2739

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend for one year the increased deduction for start-up expenditures.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Jump Start American Small Business Innovation Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Extension of increased deduction for start-up expenditures

- **(a)** *In general* Paragraph (3) of section 195(b) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended—
  - **(1)** in the text by striking “2010” and inserting “2010 or 2011”, and
  - **(2)** in the heading by striking “2010” and inserting “2010 or 2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to amounts paid or incurred in taxable years beginning after December 31, 2010.

# H.R. 4076

> To amend the Truth in Lending Act to add a rule of construction relating to certain payments to an employee of a mortgage originator.

## § 1. Rule of construction

Section 129B(c)(4) of the Truth in Lending Act is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (C), by striking “or” at the end;
- **(2)** in subparagraph (D), by striking the period and inserting “; or”; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following new subparagraph:
  > - **(E)** prohibiting a creditor or mortgage originator from reducing compensation to an employee mortgage originator if, after an initial annual percentage rate (“APR”) offer has been disclosed to a consumer by the employee mortgage originator, such employee mortgage originator makes a subsequent offer to the consumer that—
  >   - **(i)** consists only of a reduction in the APR from the initial disclosed APR; and
  >   - **(ii)** is made in response to a lower APR offer disclosed to the consumer by another creditor or mortgage originator.

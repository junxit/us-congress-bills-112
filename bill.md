# H.R. 6328

That the bill from the House of Representatives (H.R. 6328) entitled “An Act to amend title 49, United States Code, to direct the Assistant Secretary of Homeland Security (Transportation Security Administration) to transfer unclaimed clothing recovered at airport security checkpoints to local veterans organizations and other local charitable organizations, and for other purposes.”, do pass with the following

*On page 2, line 20, after “clothing to” insert “the local airport authority or other local authorities for donation to charity, including”.*

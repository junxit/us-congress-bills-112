# S. 1182

> To prohibit the further extension or establishment of national monuments in Utah except by express authorization of Congress.

## § 1. Limitation on further extension or establishment of national monuments in Utah

This proviso of the last sentence of the first section of the Act of September 14, 1950 (64 Stat. 849, chapter 950; 16 U.S.C. 431a), is amended by inserting “or Utah” after “Wyoming”.

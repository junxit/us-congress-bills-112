# H.R. 6018

> To authorize appropriations for the Department of State for fiscal year 2013, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Foreign Relations Authorization Act, Fiscal Year 2013”.

## § 3. Appropriate congressional committees defined

Except as otherwise provided in this Act, the term “appropriate congressional committees” means the Committee on Foreign Affairs of the House of Representatives and the Committee on Foreign Relations of the Senate.

# H.R. 4552

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on certain acrylic or modacrylic staple fibers.

## § 1. Certain acrylic or modacrylic staple fibers

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.10.22 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to certain acrylic or modacrylic staple fibers) is amended by striking “12/31/2012” and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) apply with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.

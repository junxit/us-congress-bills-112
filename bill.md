# H.R. 6589

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 321 East California Street in Gainesville, Texas, as the “Brig. Gen. Robert E. Galer Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Brig. Gen. Robert E. Galer Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 321 East California Street in Gainesville, Texas, shall be known and designated as the “Brig. Gen. Robert E. Galer Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Brig. Gen. Robert E. Galer Post Office Building”.

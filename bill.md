# H.R. 961

> To amend title 18, United States Code, with respect to the prohibition on disrupting military funerals, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Safe Haven for Heros Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Amendments to prohibition on disrupting military funerals

Section 1388 of title 18, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a), by striking “60 minutes before and ending 60 minutes after” and inserting “5 hours before, during, and ending 5 hours after”.

# S. 11

> To provide permanent tax relief from the marriage penalty.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Permanent Marriage Penalty Relief Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Repeal of sunset on marriage penalty relief

Title IX of the Economic Growth and Tax Relief Reconciliation Act of 2001 (relating to sunset of provisions of such Act) shall not apply to sections 301, 302, and 303(a) of such Act (relating to marriage penalty relief).

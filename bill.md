# S. 2786

> To extend and modify the temporary suspension of duty on 1,3-diisocyanatomethylbenzene, polymer with 1,6- diisocyanatohexane.

## § 1. 1,3-Diisocyanatomethylbenzene, polymer with 1,6- diisocyanatohexane

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.12.18 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to 1,3-diisocyanatomethylbenzene, polymer with 1,6- diisocyanatohexane) is amended—
  - **(1)** in the article description—
    - **(A)** by striking “CAS No. 63368–95–6” and inserting “CAS No. 26426–91–5”; and
    - **(B)** by striking “%” at the end; and
  - **(2)** by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) apply to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.

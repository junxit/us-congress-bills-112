# S. 3156

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on acrylic filament tow imported in the form of bundles of crimped product each containing 214,000 filaments (plus or minus 10 percent).

## § 1. Acrylic filament tow imported in the form of bundles of crimped product each containing 214,000 filaments (plus or minus 10 percent)

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.40.22 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to acrylic filament tow imported in the form of bundles of crimped product each containing 214,000 filaments (plus or minus 10 percent)) is amended by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.

# S. RES. 340

> To amend the Standing Rules of the Senate to prohibit a Member, officer, or employee of the Senate from disclosing or using any material nonpublic information learned during the course of his or her service for personal gain.

## § 1. Amendment to the Standing Rules of the Senate

Rule XXXVII of the Standing Rules of the Senate is amended by—

- **(1)** redesignating paragraph 15 as paragraph 16; and
- **(2)** inserting after paragraph 14 the following:
  > - **(15)** A Member, officer, or employee of the Senate shall not disclose or use any material nonpublic information learned during the course of his or her service for personal gain.

# H. RES. 596

> Requesting return of official papers on H.R. 5.

That the Clerk of the House of Representatives request the Senate to return to the House the bill (H.R. 5) entitled “An Act to improve patient access to health care services and provide improved medical care by reducing the excessive burden the liability system places on the health care delivery system.”.

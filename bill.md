# H. CON. RES. 81

> Directing the Clerk of the House of Representatives to make a correction in the enrollment of H.R. 2608.

That, in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 2608) to provide for an additional temporary extension of programs under the Small Business Act and the Small Business Investment Act of 1958, and for other purposes, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the following correction:

Amend the title so as to read: “An Act making continuing appropriations for fiscal year 2012, and for other purposes.”.

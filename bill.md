# H.R. 5095

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on AC electric motors of an output exceeding 74.6 W but not exceeding 85 W.

## § 1. AC electric motors of an output exceeding 74.6 W but not exceeding 85 W

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.85.10 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to AC electric motors of an output exceeding 74.6 W but not exceeding 85 W) is amended by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.

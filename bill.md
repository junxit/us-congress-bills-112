# H.R. 4519

> To extend and modify the temporary reduction of duty on Azoxystrobin.

## § 1. Azoxystrobin

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.02.06 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to Azoxystrobin) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “5.5%” and inserting “6.2%”; and
  - **(2)** by striking “12/31/2012” and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) apply with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.

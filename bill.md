# S. RES. 596

> Permitting the solicitation of donations in Senate buildings for the relief of victims of Superstorm Sandy.

## § 1. Solicitation for Superstorm Sandy Relief

Notwithstanding any other provision of the rules or regulations of the Senate—

- **(1)** a Senator, officer of the Senate, or employee of the Senate may solicit another Senator, officer of the Senate, or employee of the Senate within Senate buildings for nonmonetary donations for the relief of victims of Superstorm Sandy during the 30-day period beginning on the date on which the Senate agrees to this resolution; and
- **(2)** a Senator, officer of the Senate, or employee of the Senate may work with a nonprofit organization with respect to the delivery of donations described in paragraph (1).

# S. 1705

> To designate the Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center in Spokane, Washington, as the “Mann-Grandstaff Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center”.

## § 1. Designation of Mann-Grandstaff Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center

- **(a)** *Designation* The Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center in Spokane, Washington, shall after the date of the enactment of this Act be known and designated as the “Mann-Grandstaff Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in any law, regulation, map, document, record, or other paper of the United States to the medical center referred to in subsection (a) shall be considered to be a reference to the Mann-Grandstaff Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center.

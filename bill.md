# S. 2619

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on 1,3-benzenedicarbonitrile.

## § 1. 1,3-benzenedicarbonitrile

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.12.06 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to 1,3-benzenedicarbonitrile) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “3.4%” in the general rate of duty column and inserting “Free”; and
  - **(2)** by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) applies with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.

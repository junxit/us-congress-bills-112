# H.R. 2244

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 67 Castle Street in Geneva, New York, as the “Corporal Steven Blaine Riccione Post Office”.

## § 1. Corporal Steven Blaine Riccione Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 67 Castle Street in Geneva, New York, shall be known and designated as the “Corporal Steven Blaine Riccione Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Corporal Steven Blaine Riccione Post Office”.

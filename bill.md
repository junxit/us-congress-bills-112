# S. 3664

> To provide for debt limit extensions.

## § 1. Debt Limit Extension

Subsection (b) of section 3101 of title 31, United States Code, is amended by striking “as provided by law through the congressional budget process described in Rule XLIX of the Rules of the House of Representatives or as provided by section 3101A or otherwise.” and inserting

> as provided—
> - **(1)** by law through the congressional budget process described in Rule XLIX of the Rules of the House of Representatives or as provided by section 3101A or otherwise, or
> - **(2)** by Executive order of the President.

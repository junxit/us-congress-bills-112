# H.R. 4627

> To extend and make a technical correction to the temporary suspension of duty on certain cast stainless steel single-piece exhaust gas manifolds.

## § 1. Certain cast stainless steel single-piece exhaust gas manifolds

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.40.94 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to certain cast stainless steel single-piece exhaust gas manifolds) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “9902.01.50” and inserting “8409.91.50”; and
  - **(2)** by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.

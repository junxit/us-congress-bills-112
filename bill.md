# H.R. 2933

> To amend title 17, United States Code, to remove the exclusion from Federal copyright of sound recordings fixed before February 15, 1972.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Sound Recording Simplification Act”.

## § 2. Removal of exclusion

Section 301 of title 17, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking subsection (c); and
- **(2)** by redesignating subsections (d), (e), and (f) as subsections (c), (d), and (e), respectively.

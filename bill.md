# S. 3366

> To designate the Haqqani network as a foreign terrorist organization.

## § 1. Designation

The Secretary of State shall designate the Haqqani network as a foreign terrorist organization under section 219 of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1189).

# H.R. 5837

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 26 East Genesee Street in Baldwinsville, New York, as the “Corporal Kyle Schneider Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Corporal Kyle Schneider Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 26 East Genesee Street in Baldwinsville, New York, shall be known and designated as the “Corporal Kyle Schneider Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Corporal Kyle Schneider Post Office Building”.

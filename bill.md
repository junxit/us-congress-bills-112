# H.R. 4931

> To extend and modify the temporary reduction of duty on 3,3′-Dichlorobenzidine dihydrochloride ([1,1′-biphenyl]- 4,4′-diamino, 3,3′-dichloro-).

## § 1. 3,3′-Dichlorobenzidine dihydrochloride ([1,1′-biphenyl]- 4,4′-diamino, 3,3′-dichloro-)

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.25.73 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to 3,3′-Dichlorobenzidine dihydrochloride ([1,1′-biphenyl]- 4,4′-diamino, 3,3′-dichloro-)) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “5.9%” and inserting “3.8%”; and
  - **(2)** by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.

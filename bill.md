# H.R. 2763

> To amend section 402(a)(2)(M) of the Personal Responsibility and Work Opportunity Reconciliation Act of 1996 to extend by two years the special rule relating to eligibility for benefits under the supplemental security income program for certain aliens and victims of trafficking.

## § 1. Extension of SSI eligibility for certain aliens and victims of trafficking

Section 402(a)(2)(M) of the Personal Responsibility and Work Opportunity Reconciliation Act of 1996 (8 U.S.C. 1612(a)(2)(M)) is amended—

- **(1)** in the subparagraph heading, by striking “2011” and inserting “2013”; and
- **(2)** in each of clauses (i)(I) and (ii), by striking “2011” and inserting “2013”.

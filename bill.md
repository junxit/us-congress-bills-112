# S. 2052

> To amend title 5, United States Code, to provide that the legal public holiday for the birthday of George Washington take place on February 22, rather than on the third Monday in February.

## § 1. Holidays

Section 6103(a) of title 5, United States Code, is amended by striking “the third Monday in February” and inserting “February 22”.

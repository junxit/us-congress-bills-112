# S. 2272

> To designate a mountain in the State of Alaska as Mount Denali.

## § 1. Designation of Mount Denali in the State of Alaska

- **(a)** *In general* The mountain located at 63° 04′ 12″ N, by 151° 00′ 18″ W, in the State of Alaska shall be known and designated as “Mount Denali”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the mountain described in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to “Mount Denali”.

# H.R. 6512

> To amend the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 to require nationally recognized statistical rating organizations to certify that they monitor the accuracy of each credit ratings issued by the organization.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Credible Ratings Act of 2012”.

## § 2. Procedures and methodologies attestation requirement

Section 15E(c)(3)(B) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (15 U.S.C. 78o–7(c)(3)(B)) is amended—

- **(1)** in clause (ii), by striking “and” at the end;
- **(2)** by redesignating clause (iii) as clause (iv); and
- **(3)** by inserting after clause (ii) the following:
  > - **(iii)** a certification that the nationally recognized statistical rating organization monitors credit ratings issued by the organization, on at least a quarterly basis, to ensure that they are as accurate as possible based on the organization’s procedures and methodologies furnished to the Commission pursuant to subsection (a)(1)(B)(ii), as applied to the most recently available data; and

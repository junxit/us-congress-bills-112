# S. 2948

> To extend the temporary reduction of duty on Aspirin.

## § 1. Aspirin

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.12.11 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to Aspirin) is amended by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.

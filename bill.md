# H.R. 3370

> To provide that the United States Postal Service may not close any post office that does not have another post office within 8 miles as measured by public roads with regular year-round access, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Protecting Our Rural Post Offices Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Limitation on closing of post offices

Section 404(d) of title 39, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(7)** Notwithstanding any other provision of this subsection, in making a determination under subsection (a)(3) as to the necessity for the closing or consolidation of a post office, the Postal Service may not close any post office that does not have another post office within 8 miles as measured by public roads with regular year-round access.

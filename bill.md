# H.R. 5332

> To prohibit agency restrictions on conference locations.

## § 1. No restriction on conference location

- **(a)** *In general* An agency may not prohibit the selection of a particular location in the United States for a conference.
- **(b)** *Definitions* In this section:
  - **(1)** *Agency* The term agency has the meaning given that term in section 5701 of title 5, United States Code.
  - **(2)** *Conference* The term conference means a meeting that is sponsored by an agency.

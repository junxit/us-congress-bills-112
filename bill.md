# S. 151

> To eliminate certain provisions relating to Texas and the Education Jobs Fund.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fairness for Texas Schools Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Elimination of provisions relating to Texas

Section 101 of Public Law 111–226 (124 Stat. 2389) is amended by striking paragraph (11).

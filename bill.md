# H.R. 2302

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to notify Congress of conferences sponsored by the Department of Veterans Affairs.

## § 1. Notification to Congress of conferences sponsored by the Department of Veterans Affairs

- **(a)** *In general* Subchapter I of chapter 5 of title 38, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new section:
  > ## § 517. Notification to Congress of conferences sponsored by the Department
  >
  > - **(a)** *Notification* Not later than 180 days before the date on which a covered conference begins, the Secretary shall notify the Committee on Veterans’ Affairs of the House of Representatives and the Committee on Veterans’ Affairs of the Senate of such conference, including the estimated costs to the Department.
  > - **(b)** *Report on costs* Not later than 60 days after the date on which a covered conference ends, the Secretary shall submit to the Committee on Veterans’ Affairs of the House of Representatives and the Committee on Veterans’ Affairs of the Senate a report that includes an accounting of the final costs of the conference to the Department.
  > - **(c)** *Covered conference defined* In this section, the term covered conference means a conference, meeting, or other similar forum that is sponsored or co-sponsored by the Department of Veterans Affairs and is—
  >   - **(1)** held for a period of three or more days, beginning at the time of initial on-site registration and ending at the time the final event is completed;
  >   - **(2)** attended by 20 or more individuals, including one or more employees of the Department; or
  >   - **(3)** estimated to cost the Department at least $5,000, including costs related to—
  >     - **(A)** transportation and parking;
  >     - **(B)** per diem payments;
  >     - **(C)** lodging;
  >     - **(D)** rental of halls, auditoriums, or other spaces;
  >     - **(E)** rental of equipment;
  >     - **(F)** refreshments;
  >     - **(G)** entertainment;
  >     - **(H)** contractors; and
  >     - **(I)** brochures or other printed media.

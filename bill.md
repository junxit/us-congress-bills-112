# S. 1008

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to permanently extend the depreciation rules for property used predominantly within an Indian reservation.

## § 1. Permanent extension of depreciation rules for property on Indian reservations

- **(a)** *In general* Subsection (j) of section 168 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to property on Indian reservations) is amended by striking paragraph (8).
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to property placed in service after December 31, 2011.

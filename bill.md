# S. 2799

> To extend the temporary reduction of duty on rubber basketballs.

## § 1. Rubber basketballs

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.13.09 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to rubber basketballs) is amended by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.

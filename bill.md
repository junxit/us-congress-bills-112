# H.R. 1186

> To repeal changes made by health care reform laws to the Medicare exception to the prohibition on certain physician referrals for hospitals.

## § 1. Repeal of health care reform provisions limiting Medicare exception to the prohibition on certain physician referrals for hospitals

Sections 6001 and 10601 of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act (Public Law 111–148) and section 1106 of the Health Care and Education Reconciliation Act of 2010 (Public Law 111–152) are repealed and the provisions of law amended by such sections are restored as if such sections had never been enacted.

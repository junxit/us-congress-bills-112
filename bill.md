# S. 2307

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on yarn of carded cashmere of 19.35 metric yarn count or higher.

## § 1. Yarn of carded cashmere of 19.35 metric yarn count or higher

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.03.02 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to yarn of carded cashmere of 19.35 metric yarn count or higher) is amended by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.

## § 2. Definitions

In this Act:

- **(1)** *Administrator* The term Administrator means the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency.
- **(2)** *Council* The term Council means the National Energy Security Council established by section 102.
- **(3)** *National energy security program* The term national energy security program means the national energy security program established by section 101.
- **(4)** *National oil independence goal* The term national oil independence goal means the national oil independence goal established under section 101(c).
- **(5)** *National oil independence plan* The term national oil independence plan means the national oil independence plan established under section 101(d).

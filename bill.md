# S. 2545

> To amend the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States to clarify the duty on sanitary towels and tampons, diapers and diaper liners for babies, and similar articles, of any material.

## § 1. Technical correction relating to duty on sanitary towels and tampons, diapers and diaper liners for babies, and similar articles, of any material

- **(a)** *In general* Subheading 9619.00 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to sanitary towels and tampons, diapers and diaper liners for babies, and similar articles, of any material) is amended by striking subheadings 9619.00.05 through 9619.00.90 and inserting the following new subheadings, with the article descriptions for such subheadings having the same degree of indentation as the article description for subheading 9616.10.00:
- **(b)** *Special duty rate staging* The amendments made by subsection (a) shall not be construed to affect the special duty rate staging with respect to articles imported from Australia proclaimed before the date of the enactment of this Act.
- **(c)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) apply with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.

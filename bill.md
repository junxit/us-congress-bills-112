# S. 3671

> To provide certain assistance to North Atlantic Treaty Organization allies.

## § 1. Certain trade with NATO allies

The Foreign Assistance Act of 1961 is amended by inserting after section 667 (22 U.S.C. 2427) the following:

> ## § 668. Certain trade with NATO allies
>
> For purposes of section 3(c) of the Act of June 21, 1938, each North Atlantic Treaty Organization member nation shall be treated as a nation with which there is in effect a free trade agreement.

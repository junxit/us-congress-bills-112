# H.R. 3330

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to extend the Department of Veterans Affairs demonstration projects on adjustable rate mortgages and hybrid adjustable rate mortgages.

## § 1. Extension of Department of Veterans Affairs demonstration projects on adjustable rate mortgages and hybrid adjustable rate mortgages

- **(a)** *Adjustable rate mortgages* Section 3707(a) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “2012” and inserting “2017”.
- **(b)** *Hybrid adjustable rate mortgages* Section 3707A(a) of such title is amended by striking “2012” and inserting “2017”.

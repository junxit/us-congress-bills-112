# H.R. 2936

> To amend the Small Business Jobs Act of 2010 with respect to the Small Business Administration’s Express Loan Program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Small Business Administration Express Loan Extension Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Temporary express loan enhancement

Section 1135(b) of the Small Business Jobs Act of 2010 is amended by striking “Effective 1 year after” and inserting “On the date that is 2 years after”.

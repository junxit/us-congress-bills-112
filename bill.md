# H.R. 850

> To facilitate a proposed project in the Lower St. Croix Wild and Scenic River, and for other purposes.

## § 1. St. croix river crossing project

Construction of a four-lane highway bridge over the Lower St. Croix River, in accordance with the Section 7 Evaluation issued by the National Park Service in October 2005, is hereby deemed to be consistent with the Wild and Scenic Rivers Act (16 U.S.C. 1271 et seq.).

# S. 1426

> To authorize certain authorities by the Department of State, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Foreign Relations Authorization Act, Fiscal Years 2012 and 2013”.

## § 3. Definitions

In this Act:

- **(1)** *Appropriate congressional committees* Except as provided in section 503(c), the term appropriate congressional committees means the Committee on Foreign Relations of the Senate and the Committee on Foreign Affairs of the House of Representatives.
- **(2)** *Secretary* Except as otherwise provided, the term Secretary means the Secretary of State.

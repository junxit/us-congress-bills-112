# H.R. 5812

> To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to extend the reduced interest rate for Federal Direct Stafford Loans.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Investing in Our Student's Future Act”.

## § 2. Interest rate extension

Subparagraph (D) of section 455(b)(7) of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1087e(b)(7)) is amended—

- **(1)** in the matter preceding clause (i), by striking “and before July 1, 2012,”; and
- **(2)** in clause (v), by striking “and before July 1, 2012,”.

## § 3. One-percent increase in highest marginal rate of tax for individuals

Section 1 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by adding at the end the following new subsection:

> - **(j)** *One-Percent increase in highest marginal rate* In the case of taxable years beginning in a calendar year after 2012, the highest marginal rate of tax in effect under subsections (a), (b), (c), and (d) for the taxable year shall be increased by 1 percentage point.

# H.R. 2526

> To exempt National Forest System lands in Alaska from the Roadless Area Conservation Rule.

## § 1. Roadless Area Conservation Rule exemption

Sections 294.10 through 294.18 of title 36, Code of Federal Regulations, known as the Roadless Area Conservation Rule, do not apply with respect to any National Forest System lands in Alaska.

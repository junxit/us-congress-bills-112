# H.R. 3396

> To abolish the Office of Polar Programs of the National Science Foundation, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Office of Polar Programs

The Office of Polar Programs of the National Science Foundation is abolished, and any unobligated funds appropriated for the activities of such office are rescinded and shall be returned to the Treasury to reduce the Federal deficit.

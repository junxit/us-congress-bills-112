# H. CON. RES. 80

> Expressing the sense of Congress that the United Nations or NATO should investigate the treatment of black Africans in Libya.

That it is the sense of Congress that the Secretary of State should direct the permanent representative of the United States to the North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) and the permanent representative of the United States to the United Nations to request that NATO and the United Nations, respectively, investigate the treatment of black Africans in Libya.

# H.R. 4037

> To provide that no Federal funds may be used for any construction project in the Northern Mariana Islands the cost of which exceeds $100,000, unless the workforce carrying out the project is composed of at least 60 percent United States workers.

## § 1. Workforce requirement for construction projects funded with Federal funds

No Federal funds may be used for any construction project in the Northern Mariana Islands the cost of which exceeds $100,000, unless the workforce carrying out the project is composed of at least 60 percent United States workers (as that term is defined in section 214.2(w)(xii) of title 8, Code of Federal Regulations).

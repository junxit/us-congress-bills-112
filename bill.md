# H.R. 5957

> To prohibit the Secretary of Homeland Security from granting deferred action or otherwise suspending the effectiveness or enforcement of the immigration laws.

## § 1. Prohibition on granting deferred action

Beginning on the date of the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of Homeland Security may not grant deferred action or otherwise suspend the effectiveness or enforcement of the immigration laws (as defined in section 101(a)(17) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1101(a)(17))).

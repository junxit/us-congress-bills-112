# S.J. RES. 17

> Approving the renewal of import restrictions contained in the Burmese Freedom and Democracy Act of 2003.

That Congress approves the renewal of the import restrictions contained in section 3(a)(1) and section 3A(b)(1) and (c)(1) of the Burmese Freedom and Democracy Act of 2003.

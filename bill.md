# H.R. 3441

> To repeal the Department of Energy’s weatherization assistance program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stop Green Initiative Abuse Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Repeal of Weatherization Assistance Program of the Department of Energy

Part A of title IV of the Energy Conservation and Production Act (42 U.S.C. 6861 et seq.), and the items relating thereto in the table of contents of such Act, are repealed.

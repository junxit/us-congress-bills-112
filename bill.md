# H.R. 3050

> To amend title 23, United States Code, to eliminate the requirement that States spend a certain amount of their funds for transportation enhancement activities.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Transportation Funding Flexibility Act”.

## § 2. Eliminate transportation enhancement activity spending requirement

Section 133(d) of title 23, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking paragraph (2); and
- **(2)** in paragraph (5)(A) by striking “funded from the allocation required under paragraph (2)”.

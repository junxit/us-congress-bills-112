# H.R. 274

> To rename the Homestead National Monument of America near Beatrice, Nebraska, as the Homestead National Historical Park.

## § 1. Homestead National Historical Park

- **(a)** *Renaming* The unit of the National Park System known as the Homestead National Monument of America and located near Beatrice, Nebraska, shall be known as the “Homestead National Historical Park”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the unit of the National Park System known as the Homestead National Monument of America shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Homestead National Historical Park”.

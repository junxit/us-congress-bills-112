# H.R. 3920

> To extend temporarily the suspension of duty on Acid Black 172.

## § 1. Acid Black 172

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.01.75 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended by striking “12/31/2012” and inserting “12/31/2014”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.

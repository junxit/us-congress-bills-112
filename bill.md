# H.R. 680

> To prohibit United States contributions to the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change.

## § 1. Prohibition on United States contributions to the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change

- **(a)** *Prohibition* Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the President may not make contributions on behalf of the United States to the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC).
- **(b)** *Effective date* The prohibition contained in subsection (a) applies with respect to fiscal year 2012 and subsequent fiscal years.

# H.R. 2286

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide tax credit parity for electricity produced from renewable resources.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Renewable Energy Parity Act”.

## § 2. Tax credit parity for electricity produced from renewable resources

- **(a)** *In general* Subparagraph (A) of section 45(b)(4) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by inserting “and before 2012” after “any calendar year after 2003”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to electricity produced and sold after December 31, 2011.

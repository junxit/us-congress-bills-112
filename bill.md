# H.R. 3745

> To amend the Federal Credit Union Act with respect to the limitations on member business loans.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Faith-Based Lending Protection Act”.

## § 2. Member business loan exclusion for loans to nonprofit religious organizations

Section 107A(a) of the Federal Credit Union Act (12 U.S.C. 1757a(a)) is amended by inserting “, excluding loans made to nonprofit religious organizations,” after “total amount of such loans”.

# H.R. 6718

> To reauthorize part C of the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Early Intervention for Toddlers and Infants with Disabilities Act”.

## § 2. Infants and toddlers with disabilities

Section 644 of the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (20 U.S.C. 1444) is amended by striking “fiscal years 2005 through 2010” and inserting “fiscal years 2014 through 2019”.

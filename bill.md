# S. 2292

> To promote accountability, transparency, innovation, efficiency, and timeliness at the Food and Drug Administration for America's patients.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Promoting Accountability, Transparency, Innovation, Efficiency, and Timeliness at FDA Act of 2012” or the “PATIENTS' FDA Act”.

# H.R. 1215

> To amend title V of the Social Security Act to convert funding for personal responsibility education programs from direct appropriations to an authorization of appropriations.

## § 1. Converting funding for personal responsibility education programs from direct appropriations to an authorization of appropriations

- **(a)** *In general* Section 513(f) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 713(f)), as added by section 2953 of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act (Public Law 111–148) and as amended by section 10201(h) of such Act, is amended—
  - **(1)** in the heading, by striking “Appropriation” and inserting “Authorization of appropriations”;
  - **(2)** by striking “appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated,” and inserting “authorized to be appropriated”;
  - **(3)** by striking “2010” and inserting “2012”; and
  - **(4)** by striking the second sentence.
- **(b)** *Conforming amendments* Section 513 of the Social Security Act is further amended—
  - **(1)** in subsection (a)(1)—
    - **(A)** in subparagraph (A)(i), by striking “amount” and inserting “amounts”; and
    - **(B)** by striking subparagraph (B) and redesignating subparagraph (C) as subparagraph (B); and
  - **(2)** in subsection (c)—
    - **(A)** in paragraph (1), by striking “amount” and inserting “amounts”; and
    - **(B)** in paragraph (2), by striking “amount” and inserting “amounts”.
- **(c)** *Rescission of unobligated funds* Of the funds made available under such section 513, the unobligated balance is rescinded.

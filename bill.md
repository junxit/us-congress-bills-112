# H.R. 3149

> To amend title I of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act to expand access to high risk pools.

## § 1. Expanding access to high risk pools

Section 1101(d)(2) of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act (Public Law 111–148) is amended by—

- **(1)** inserting “(A)” after “(2)”;
- **(2)** striking “; and” at the end and inserting “; or”; and
- **(3)** and inserting at the end the following:
  > - **(B)** has been covered under a State health benefits risk pool (including a “qualified high risk pool”, as such term is defined in section 2744(c)(2) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 300gg–44(c)(2))) during such 6-month period; and

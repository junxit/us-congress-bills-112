# S. 1548

> To extend the National Flood Insurance Program until December 31, 2011.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “National Flood Insurance Program Extension Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Extension of the national flood insurance program

- **(a)** *Program extension* Section 1319 of the National Flood Insurance Act of 1968 (42 U.S.C. 4026) is amended by striking “September 30, 2011” and inserting “December 31, 2011”.
- **(b)** *Financing* Section 1309(a) of the National Flood Insurance Act of 1968 (42 U.S.C. 4016(a)) is amended by striking “September 30, 2011” and inserting “December 31, 2011”.

# H. RES. 516

> Expressing the sense of the House of Representatives that the passage of a fiscal year 2013 Federal budget is of national importance.

## Preamble

- Whereas the Congressional Budget Act of 1974 established the modern budgeting process;
- Whereas the President is required to submit a budget to Congress each year;
- Whereas the last time the House of Representatives passed a budget was on April 15, 2011;
- Whereas the last time the Senate passed a budget was on April 29, 2009; and
- Whereas people in the United States must routinely set budgets for themselves, their businesses, and their families: Now, therefore, be it

That it is the sense of the House of Representatives that the passage of a fiscal year 2013 Federal budget is of national importance.

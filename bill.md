# S. 2560

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on certain bags for toys.

## § 1. Extension of duty suspension on certain bags for toys

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.01.78 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to certain bags for toys) is amended by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.

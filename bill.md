# H.R. 4493

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on Bromoxynil Octanoate.

## § 1. Bromoxynil Octanoate

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.22.97 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to Bromoxynil Octanoate) is amended by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.

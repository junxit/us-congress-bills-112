# H. RES. 839

> Relating to the death of the Honorable Daniel K. Inouye, a Senator from the State of Hawaii.

That the House of Representatives—

- **(1)** receives with profound sorrow the news of the death of the Honorable Daniel K. Inouye, a Senator from the State of Hawaii;
- **(2)** authorizes the Speaker to appoint such Members as he may designate to serve with members of the Senate as a committee to represent the House in attendance at the funeral of the Senator;
- **(3)** directs the Clerk to communicate this resolution to the Senate and transmit a copy to the family of the Senator; and
- **(4)** when it adjourns today, does so as a further mark of respect to the memory of the Senator.

# S. 1043

> To amend the Energy Independence and Security Act of 2007 to promote energy security through the production of petroleum from oil sands, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Oil Sands Energy Security Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Procurement and acquisition of alternative fuels

Section 526 of the Energy Independence and Security Act of 2007 (42 U.S.C. 17142) is repealed.

# H.R. 5802

> To amend title 46, United States Code, to authorize use of port security grant funds for replacement of certain security equipment or facilities.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Port Security Equipment Improvement Act of 2012”.

## § 2. Use of port security grant funds for replacement of security equipment or facilities

Section 70107(b)(2) of title 46, United States Code, is amended by striking “and maintenance” and inserting “maintenance, and replacement”.

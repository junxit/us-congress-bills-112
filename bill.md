# H.J. RES. 83

> Disapproving the issuance of a letter of offer with respect to a certain proposed sale of defense articles and defense services to Turkey.

That the issuance of a letter of offer with respect to the proposed sale of defense articles and defense services to Turkey (described in the certifications transmitted to the Speaker of the House of Representatives and the Chairman of the Committee on Foreign Relations of the Senate pursuant to section 36(b) of the Arms Export Control Act on October 28, 2011 (Transmittal Number 11–31)) is hereby prohibited.

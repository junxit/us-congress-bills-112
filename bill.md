# H.R. 5350

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on Dinotefuran.

## § 1. Dinotefuran

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.24.30 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to Dinotefuran) is amended by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.

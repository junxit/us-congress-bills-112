# S. 1402

> To amend the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972 to increase the maximum penalty for violating that Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Marine Mammal Protection Amendment Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Penalties under the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972

Section 105(b) of the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972 (16 U.S.C. 1375(b)) is amended by striking “$20,000” and inserting “$50,000”.

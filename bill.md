# H.R. 2518

> To extend for 5 years the authorization of appropriations for the sickle cell disease prevention and treatment demonstration program.

## § 1. 5-year extension of authorization of appropriations for the sickle cell disease prevention and treatment demonstration program

Section 712(c)(6) of the American Jobs Creation Act of 2004 (Public Law 108–357; 42 U.S.C. 300b–1 note) is amended by striking “each of fiscal years 2005 through 2009” and inserting “each of fiscal years 2013 through 2017”.

# H. RES. 515

> Addressing a motion to proceed under section 3101A of title 31, United States Code.

That a motion to proceed with regard to a joint resolution of disapproval specified in subsection (a)(2) of section 3101A of title 31, United States Code—

- **(a)** may be offered even if the joint resolution has not been reported to the House as contemplated by subsection (c)(3) of such section; and
- **(b)** shall be in order only if offered by the Majority Leader or his designee.

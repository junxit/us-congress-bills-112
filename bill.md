# S. 1756

> To extend HUBZone designations by 3 years, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “HUBZone Protection Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Redesignated areas

Section 3(p)(4)(C)(i) of the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 632(p)(4)(C)(i)) is amended to read as follows:

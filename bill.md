# H.R. 5488

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on low expansion laboratory glassware, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Low expansion laboratory glassware

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.12.39 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended—
  - **(1)** in the article description column, by striking “10−7” and inserting “10−6”; and
  - **(2)** in effective period column, by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section apply to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.

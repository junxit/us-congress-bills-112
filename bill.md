# H.R. 1757

> To make permanent the estate tax provisions enacted as part of the Tax Relief, Unemployment Insurance Reauthorization, and Job Creation Act of 2010.

## § 1. Estate tax provisions of Tax Relief, Unemployment Insurance Reauthorization, and Job Creation Act of 2010 made permanent

- **(a)** *In general* Title III of the Tax Relief, Unemployment Insurance Reauthorization, and Job Creation Act of 2010 is amended by striking section 304 (relating to application of EGTRRA sunset to this title).
- **(b)** *Conforming amendment* Section 901 of the Economic Growth and Tax Relief Reconciliation Act of 2001 is amended—
  - **(1)** in subsection (a) by striking “apply—” and all that follows and inserting “to taxable, plan, or limitation years beginning after December 31, 2012”, and
  - **(2)** in subsection (b) by striking “years, estates, gifts, and transfers” and inserting “years”.

# H.R. 4347

> To designate the United States courthouse located at 709 West 9th Street in Juneau, Alaska, as the “Robert Boochever United States Courthouse”.

## § 1. Designation

The United States courthouse located at 709 West 9th Street in Juneau, Alaska, shall be known and designated as the “Robert Boochever United States Courthouse”.

## § 2. References

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the United States courthouse referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Robert Boochever United States Courthouse”.

# H.R. 5867

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 4605 Tutu Park Mall in St. Thomas, United States Virgin Islands, as the “Kenneth Leslie Hermon Post Office”.

## § 1. Kenneth Leslie Hermon Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 4605 Tutu Park Mall in St. Thomas, United States Virgin Islands, shall be known and designated as the “Kenneth Leslie Hermon Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Kenneth Leslie Hermon Post Office”.

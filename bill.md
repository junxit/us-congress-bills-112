# S. 291

> To repeal the sunset provisions in the USA PATRIOT Improvement and Reauthorization Act of 2005 and other related provisions and permanently reauthorize the USA PATRIOT Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “USA PATRIOT Reauthorization Act of 2011.”.

## § 2. USA Patriot Improvement and Reauthorization Act repeal of sunset provisions

Section 102(b) of the USA PATRIOT Improvement and Reauthorization Act of 2005 (Public Law 109–177; 50 U.S.C. 1805 note, 50 U.S.C. 1861 note, and 50 U.S.C. 1862 note) is repealed.

## § 3. Repeal of sunset relating to individual terrorists as agents of foreign powers

Section 6001(b) of the Intelligence Reform and Terrorism Prevention Act of 2004 (Public Law 108–458; 50 U.S.C. 1801 note) is repealed.

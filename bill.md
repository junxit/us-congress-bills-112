# H.R. 5379

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on certain reusable grocery bags.

## § 1. Certain reusable grocery bags

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.40.01 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to shopping bags of an outer surface of spun bonded polypropylene fabric or nonwoven polypropylene fabric) is amended by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.

# H.R. 1213

> To repeal mandatory funding provided to States in the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act to establish American Health Benefit Exchanges.

## § 1. Repealing mandatory funding to States to establish American Health Benefit Exchanges

- **(a)** *In general* Section 1311(a) of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act (42 U.S.C. 13031(a)) is repealed.
- **(b)** *Rescission of unobligated funds* Of the funds made available under such section 1311(a), the unobligated balance is rescinded.

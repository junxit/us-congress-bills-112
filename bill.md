# H.R. 3865

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the American Opportunity Tax Credit.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “College Tax Cut Extension Act of 2012”.

## § 2. Extension of American Opportunity Tax Credit

- **(a)** *In general* Subsection (i) of section 25A of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “in 2009, 2010, 2011, or 2012” and inserting “after 2008 and before 2017”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to taxable years beginning after 2012.

# H.R. 1945

> To direct the Secretary of the Navy to name the next available Naval vessel after United States Marine Corps Sergeant Rafael Peralta.

## § 1. Naming of Naval vessel after United States Marine Corps Sergeant Rafael Peralta

The Secretary of the Navy shall name the next available Naval vessel after United States Marine Corps Sergeant Rafael Peralta.

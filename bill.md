# S. 746

> To repeal provisions of the Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Dodd-Frank Repeal Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Repeal

Effective on the date of enactment of this Act, titles I through XV of the Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act (Public Law 111–203) are repealed, and the provisions of law amended or repealed by such titles are restored or revived as if such Act had not been enacted.

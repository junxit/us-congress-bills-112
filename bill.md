# H.R. 3295

> To amend the charter of the Archaeological Institute of America with respect to the principal office of the corporation.

## § 1. Amendment to Federal charter of Archaeological Institute of America

Section 3 of the Act entitled “An Act incorporating the Archaeological Institute of America”, approved May 26, 1906 (34 Stat. 203), as amended by Public Law 994 of the 84th Congress (70 Stat. 1060), is amended by striking “Washington, in the District of Columbia,” and inserting “such place in the United States as the corporation may determine,”.

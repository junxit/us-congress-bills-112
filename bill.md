# S. 2509

> To suspend temporarily the duty on programmable controllers certified by the importer as designed for use in agricultural and off-road construction vehicles to control vehicle accessories and auxiliary functions.

## § 1. Programmable controllers certified by the importer as designed for use in agricultural and off-road construction vehicles to control vehicle accessories and auxiliary functions

- **(a)** *In general* Subchapter II of chapter 99 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended by inserting in numerical sequence the following new heading:
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.

# H.R. 6678

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to encourage research at community colleges and other institutions of higher education, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “College Research Education and Teacher Excellence (CREATE) Opportunity Act of 2012”.

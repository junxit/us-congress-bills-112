# H.R. 6605

> To eliminate an unnecessary reporting requirement for an unfunded DNA Identification grant program.

## § 1. Elimination of report requirement

Section 2406 of title I of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (42 U.S.C. 3796kk–5) is repealed.

# S. 2051

> To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to extend the reduced interest rate for Federal Direct Stafford Loans.

## § 1. Interest rate extension

Section 455(b)(7)(D) of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1087e(b)(7)(D)) is amended—

- **(1)** in the matter preceding clause (i), by striking “and before July 1, 2012,”; and
- **(2)** in clause (v), by striking “and before July 1, 2012,”.

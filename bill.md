# H.R. 899

> To amend title 41, United States Code, to extend the sunset date for certain protests of task and deliver order contracts.

## § 1. Extension of sunset date for certain protests of task and deliver order contracts

Paragraph (3) of section 4106(f) of title 41, United States Code, is amended to read as follows:

> - **(3)** *Effective period* Paragraph (1)(B) and paragraph (2) of this subsection shall not be in effect after September 30, 2016.

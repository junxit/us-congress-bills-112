# H.R. 3066

> To preserve the companionship services exemption for minimum wage and overtime pay under the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Companionship Exemption Protection Act”.

## § 2. Preservation of the companionship services exemption

Paragraph (15) of section 13(a) of the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 (29 U.S.C. 213(a)(15)) is amended—

- **(1)** by inserting “(including third party non-medical in-home care such as companionship, light housekeeping, meal preparation, errands, assistance to appointments, laundry, medication reminders, bathing, and assistance with incontinence and grooming)” after “companionship services”; and
- **(2)** by striking “(as such terms are defined and delimited by regulations of the Secretary)”.

# H.R. 4577

> To extend and modify the temporary reduction of duty on mixtures containing Isoxaflutole and Cyprosulfamide.

## § 1. Mixtures containing Isoxaflutole and Cyprosulfamide

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.40.65 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to mixtures containing Isoxaflutole and Cyprosulfamide) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “3.5%” and inserting “2.7%”; and
  - **(2)** by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) apply with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.

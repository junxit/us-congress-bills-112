# S. 2136

> To increase the maximum amount of leverage permitted under title III of the Small Business Investment Act of 1958, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Maximum leverage under title III of the Small Business Investment Act of 1958

- **(a)** *Authorization* For fiscal year 2013, the Administrator may make $4,000,000,000 in guarantees of debentures for programs under title III of the Small Business Investment Act of 1958 (15 U.S.C. 681 et seq.).
- **(b)** *Family of funds* Section 303(b)(2)(B) of the Small Business Investment Act of 1958 (15 U.S.C. 683(b)(2)(B)) is amended by striking “$225,000,000” and inserting “$350,000,000”.

# H.R. 4063

> To repeal section 512 of the Credit Card Accountability Responsibility and Disclosure Act of 2009 which relates to carrying certain weapons in National Parks.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Guns-Free National Parks Act of 2012”.

## § 2. Repeal of provision that relates to carrying certain weapons in National Parks

Section 512 of the Credit Card Accountability Responsibility and Disclosure Act of 2009 (16 U.S.C. 1a–7b) is repealed.

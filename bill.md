# H.R. 3518

> To impose a regulatory moratorium and prevent taxes from being raised for 2 years.

## § 1. In general

During the period beginning on the date of enactment of this Act and terminating on the date that is 2 years after that date, no agency may make any rule that would affect employment levels, and the Congress shall not enact any law resulting in an increase in the tax liability of any person or entity.

# H.R. 6592

> To ensure that the Metropolitan Washington Airports Authority complies with the Federal Acquisition Regulation.

## § 1. Application of the federal acquisition regulation to MWAA

The Metropolitan Washington Airports Authority shall comply with the Federal Acquisition Regulation.

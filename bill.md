# H.R. 1522

> To repeal the Energy Independence and Security Act of 2007.

## § 1. Repeal of Energy Independence and Security Act of 2007

The Energy Independence and Security Act of 2007 (Public Law 110–140) is repealed, and the laws amended by that Act shall be read as if the amendments made by that Act were not enacted.

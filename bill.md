# H.R. 4732

> To extend and modify the temporary reduction of duty on 4-methoxy-2-methyldiphenylamine.

## § 1. 4-Methoxy-2-methyldiphenylamine

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.11.35 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to 4-methoxy-2-methyldiphenylamine) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “1.1%” in the column 1 general rate of duty column and inserting “Free”; and
  - **(2)** by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) apply with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.

# H.R. 2270

> To amend section 1605A of title 28, United States Code, to provide that the statute of limitations must be raised as an affirmative defense.

## § 1. Statute of limitations in certain civil actions

Section 1605A(b) of title 28, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following flush sentence:

## § 2. Effective date

The amendment made by section 1 applies to any case that is pending on, or is brought on or after, the date of the enactment of this Act.

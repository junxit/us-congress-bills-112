# H.R. 262

> To require any amounts remaining in a Member’s Representational Allowance at the end of a fiscal year to be deposited in the Treasury and used to reduce the Federal debt.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Mandatory Returning of Allowances Act” or the “MRA Act”.

## § 2. Requiring amounts remaining in members’ representational allowances to be used to reduce the federal debt

- **(a)** *In General* Notwithstanding any other provision of law, any amounts appropriated for Members’ Representational Allowances for the House of Representatives for a fiscal year which remain after all payments are made under such Allowances for the year shall be deposited in the Treasury and used to reduce the Federal debt (in such manner as the Secretary of the Treasury considers appropriate).
- **(b)** *Effective Date* This section shall apply with respect to fiscal year 2011 and each succeeding fiscal year.

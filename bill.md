# H.R. 5247

> To extend and modify the temporary suspension of duty on Desmodur R-E.

## § 1. Desmodur R-E

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.11.83 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to Desmodur R-E) is amended—
  - **(1)** by amending the article description to read as follows: “Polyisocyanate cross linking agent products containing triphenylmethane triisocyanate (CAS No. 2422–91–5) in solvents (provided for in subheading 3824.90.28)”; and
  - **(2)** by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.

# H.R. 1429

> To provide for the compensation of furloughed Federal employees.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Federal Employee Retroactive Pay Fairness Act”.

## § 2. Compensation for furloughed Federal employees

Federal employees furloughed as a result of any lapse in appropriations which begins on or about April 9, 2011, shall be compensated at their standard rate of compensation, for the period of such lapse in appropriations, as soon as practicable after such lapse in appropriations ends.

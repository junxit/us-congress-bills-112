# H.R. 6536

> To authorize the President to seek to conduct negotiations with the European Union for purposes of entering into a trade agreement with the European Union, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Trade agreement authority with respect to the European Union

- **(a)** *Authority* Subject to subsection (b), the President is authorized to seek to conduct negotiations with the European Union for purposes of entering into a trade agreement with the European Union to reduce existing duties or other import restrictions of the European Union or the United States that are unduly burdening and restricting the foreign trade of the United States.
- **(b)** *Congressional notification and consultation* The exercise of the authority of subsection (a) shall be subject to all applicable congressional notification and consultation requirements provided for under any other provision of law.

# H.R. 2043

> To amend the Revised Statutes of the United States to authorize vicarious liability in certain civil actions dealing with the deprivation of rights.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Restoration of Civil Rights Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Vicarious liability

Section 1979 of the Revised Statutes (42 U.S.C. 1983) is amended by inserting after “District of Columbia,” the following: “whether directly or vicariously through any employee or agent,”.

# S. 3644

> To provide for indemnification of transferees of property at any closed military installation.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Base Redevelopment and Indemnification Correction Act of 2012”.

## § 2. Indemnification of transferees of property at any closed military installation

Section 330 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 1993 (Public Law 102–484; 10 U.S.C. 2687 note) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a)(1), by striking “pursuant to a base closure law” and inserting “after October 24, 1988, the date of the enactment of the Defense Authorization Amendments and Base Closure and Realignment Act (Public Law 100–526; 10 U.S.C. 2687 note)”; and
- **(2)** in subsection (f), by striking paragraph (3).

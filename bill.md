# H.R. 38

> To rescind funds appropriated to the Health Insurance Reform Implementation Fund under the Health Care and Education Reconciliation Act of 2010.

## § 1. Rescission of Health Insurance Reform Implementation funds

The unobligated balance of funds made available by section 1005(b) of the Health Care and Education Reconciliation Act of 2010 (Public Law 111–152; 42 U.S.C. 18121(b)) is hereby rescinded.

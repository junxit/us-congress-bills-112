# H.R. 348

> To prohibit the Administrator of the Federal Highway Administration from requiring the replacement of street and highway signs that are in upper case letters with such signs that are in mixed case lettering with the initial letter in upper case followed by lower case lettering.

## § 1. No new signs

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Administrator of the Federal Highway Administration may not require the replacement of street and highway signs that are in upper case letters with signs that are in mixed case lettering with the initial letter in upper case followed by lower case lettering.

# H.R. 3247

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 1100 Town and Country Commons in Chesterfield, Missouri, as the “Lance Corporal Matthew P. Pathenos Post Office Building”.

## § 1. Lance Corporal Matthew P. Pathenos Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 1100 Town and Country Commons in Chesterfield, Missouri, shall be known and designated as the “Lance Corporal Matthew P. Pathenos Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Lance Corporal Matthew P. Pathenos Post Office Building”.

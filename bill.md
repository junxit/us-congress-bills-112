# H.R. 4137

> To make permanent the exclusion from gross income for employer-provided educational assistance.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Employee Educational Assistance Act of 2012”.

## § 2. Exclusion for employer-provided educational assistance made permanent

Section 901 of the Economic Growth and Tax Relief Reconciliation Act of 2001 shall not apply to section 411 of such Act.

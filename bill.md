# H.R. 5804

> To amend the Homeland Security Act of 2002 to require the Administrator of the Federal Emergency Management Agency to provide guidance to and coordination with local educational agencies and school districts that are at high risk of acts of terrorism or other incidents, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Preparedness Coordination With Local Educational Agencies and School Districts Act”.

## § 2. Guidance to and coordination with local educational agencies and school districts

- **(a)** *In general* Title V of the Homeland Security Act of 2002 (6 U.S.C. 311 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following new section:
  > ## § 526. Guidance to and coordination with local educational agencies and school districts
  >
  > To enhance domestic preparedness for and collective response to terrorism and other incidents, the Administrator of the Federal Emergency Management Agency shall provide guidance to and coordinate with local educational agencies and school districts that are at a high risk of acts of terrorism or other incidents.

# H.R. 221

> To amend title 18, United States Code, to provide penalties for displaying nooses in public with intent to harass or intimidate a person because of that person’s race, color, religion, or national origin.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Noose Hate Crime Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Displaying nooses in public with intent to harass or intimidate

- **(a)** *In general* Chapter 13 of title 18, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following:
  > ## § 250. Displaying nooses in public with intent to intimidate
  >
  > Whoever, with intent to harass or intimidate any person because of that person’s race, color, religion, or national origin, displays a noose in public shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than 2 years, or both.

# H.R. 2573

> To amend section 242 of the National Housing Act to extend the period of applicability of the exemption for critical access hospitals under the FHA program for mortgage insurance for hospitals.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Rural Health Care Capital Access Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Extension of exemption for critical access hospitals

Paragraph (1) of section 242(i) of the National Housing Act (12 U.S.C. 1715z–7(i)(1)) is amended by striking “July 31, 2011” and inserting “July 31, 2016”.

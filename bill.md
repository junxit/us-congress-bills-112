# H.R. 1607

> To amend the Farm Security and Rural Investment Act of 2002 to extend the suspension of the limitation on the period for which certain borrowers are eligible for guaranteed assistance.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Creating Real Opportunities for Prosperity (CROP) Act”.

## § 2. Suspension of limitation on period for which borrowers are eligible for guaranteed assistance

Section 5102 of the Farm Security and Rural Investment Act of 2002 (7 U.S.C. 1949 note; Public Law 107–171) is amended by striking “December 31, 2010” and inserting “December 31, 2013”.

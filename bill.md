# S.J. RES. 46

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rules submitted by the Department of the Treasury and the Internal Revenue Service relating to the reporting requirements for interest that relates to deposits maintained at United States offices of certain financial institutions and is paid to certain nonresident alien individuals.

## Preamble

- Now, therefore, be it

That the Congress disapproves the rules submitted by the Department of the Treasury and the Internal Revenue Service relating to the reporting requirements for interest that relates to deposits maintained at United States offices of certain financial institutions and is paid to certain nonresident alien individuals (published at 77 Fed. Reg. 23391 (April 19, 2012)), and that such rules shall have no force or effect.

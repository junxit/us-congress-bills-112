# S. 1846

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to establish the National Veterans Support Foundation to carry out activities to support and supplement the mission of the Department of Veterans Affairs, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Nationwide Network of Support for Veterans and Their Families Act of 2011.”.

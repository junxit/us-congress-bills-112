# S. 3627

> To amend the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961 to reauthorize the Overseas Private Investment Corporation, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Overseas Private Investment Corporation Reauthorization Act of 2012”.

## § 2. Issuing authority

Section 235(a)(2) of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961 (22 U.S.C. 2195(a)(2)) is amended by striking “2007” and inserting “2015”.

# S. 3648

> To exclude from gross income for purposes of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 any payments made from the Aurora Victim Relief Fund to the victims of the tragic event at the Century 16 Cinema in Aurora, Colorado, on July 20, 2012.

## § 1. Exclusion from gross income of payments from the Aurora Victim Relief Fund

For purposes of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, gross income shall not include any amount received from the Aurora Victim Relief Fund established by the Community First Foundation, an organization organized and operated as described in section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, if such amount is paid on account of the event on July 20, 2012.

# H.R. 1747

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to make permanent the rule treating certain farming business machinery and equipment as 5-year property.

## § 1. Permanent treatment of certain farming and business machinery and equipment as 5-year property

- **(a)** *In general* Clause (vii) of section 168(e)(3)(B) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “, and which is placed in service before January 1, 2010”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to property placed in service after December 31, 2009.

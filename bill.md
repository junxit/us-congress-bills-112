# H.R. 6062

> To reauthorize the Edward Byrne Memorial Justice Assistance Grant Program through fiscal year 2017.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Edward Byrne Memorial Justice Assistance Grant Program Reauthorization Act of 2012”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of Byrne JAG grants

Section 508 of title I of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (42 U.S.C. 3758) is amended by inserting before the period the following: “, and $800,000,000 for each of the fiscal years 2013 through 2017”.

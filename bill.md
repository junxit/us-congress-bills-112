# H.R. 6499

> To amend part C of the Balanced Budget and Emergency Deficit Control Act of 1985 to include the pay of Members of Congress within the coverage of the Act.

## § 1. Definition of account

Paragraph (11) of section 250(c) of the Balanced Budget and Emergency Deficit Control Act of 1985 is amended by inserting before the period the following: “, and such term includes any item for the pay of Senators or Representatives in, or Delegates or the Resident Commissioner to, the Congress”.

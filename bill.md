# S. 195

> To reinstate Federal matching of State spending of child support incentive payments.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Child Support Protection Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Reinstatement of Federal matching of State spending of child support incentive payments

Effective October 1, 2010, section 455(a)(1) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 655(a)(1)) is amended by striking “from amounts paid to the State under section 458 or”.

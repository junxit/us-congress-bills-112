# S. 2253

> To require individuals who file under the Ethics in Government Act of 1978 to disclose any financial accounts that are or have been deposited in a country that is a tax haven.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Financial Disclosure to Reduce Tax Haven Abuse Act of 2012”.

## § 2. Disclosure of accounts held in tax havens

Section 102(b)(1) of the Ethics in Government Act of 1978 (5 U.S.C. App.) is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (A), by inserting “, with a specific accounting of any financial interest held by the covered individual or their spouse in a country that is considered as a tax haven as listed by the Secretary of the Treasury and made available to the filer” after “calendar year”; and

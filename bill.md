# H.R. 6553

> To amend the Communications Act of 1934 to add an additional purpose to section 1 of such Act.

## § 1. Additional purpose

Section 1 of the Communications Act of 1934 (47 U.S.C. 151) is amended by inserting “for the purpose of making communications networks more secure, resilient, and defendable against Internet-based attacks,” after “national defense,”.

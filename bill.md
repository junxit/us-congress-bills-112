# H.R. 3492

> To amend section 70107 of title 46, United States Code, to authorize appropriations for the port security grant program through 2015.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Port Security Grant Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Port security grant program

Section 70107(l) of title 46, United States Code, is amended by striking “2013” and inserting “2015”.

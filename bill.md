# S. 2090

> To amend the Indian Law Enforcement Reform Act to extend the period of time provided to the Indian Law and Order Commission to produce a required report, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Report of Indian Law and Order Commission

- **(a)** *In general* Section 15(f) of the Indian Law Enforcement Reform Act (25 U.S.C. 2812(f)) is amended in the matter preceding paragraph (1) by striking “2 years” and inserting “3 years”.
- **(b)** *Technical amendment* Section 15(e) of the Indian Law Enforcement Reform Act (25 U.S.C. 2812(e)) is amended in the matter preceding paragraph (1) by striking “paragraph (1)” and inserting “subsection (d)”.

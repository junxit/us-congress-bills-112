# H.R. 6010

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to increase the income limitations for the student loan interest deduction, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Increase in income limitation for student loan interest deduction

- **(a)** *In general* Subclause (II) of section 221(b)(2)(B)(i) of the Internal Revenue Code 1986 is amended by striking “$50,000 ($100,000” and inserting “$75,000 ($150,000”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to taxable years beginning after the date of the enactment of this Act.

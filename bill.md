# H.R. 4414

> To extend temporarily the reduction of duty on certain dyed polyacrylonitrile staple.

## § 1. Certain dyed polyacrylonitrile staple

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.40.30 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to certain dyed polyacrylonitrile staple) is amended by striking “12/31/2012” and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) apply with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.

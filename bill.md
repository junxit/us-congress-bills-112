# H.R. 5300

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on certain single yarns of viscose rayon.

## § 1. Certain single yarns of viscose rayon

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.54.04 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to certain single yarns of viscose rayon) is amended by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.

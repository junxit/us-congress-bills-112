# H.R. 464

> To prohibit United States contributions to the International Fund for Ireland.

## § 1. Prohibition on United States contributions to the International Fund for Ireland

- **(a)** *Prohibition* No funds appropriated or otherwise available to any Federal department or agency may be obligated or expended to make United States contributions to the International Fund for Ireland.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The prohibition contained in subsection (a) shall apply with respect to funds appropriated or otherwise made available for fiscal year 2012 and subsequent fiscal years.

# H.R. 3306

> To repeal the Advanced Technology Vehicle Manufacturing loan program.

## § 1. Advanced Technology Vehicle Manufacturing loan program

- **(a)** *Repeal* Section 136(d) of the Energy Independence and Security Act of 2007 (42 U.S.C. 17103(d)) is repealed.
- **(b)** *Effective date* This section shall take effect on the date of its enactment.

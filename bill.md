# H.R. 3538

> To amend the Railway Labor Act to direct the National Mediation Board to apply the same procedures, including voting standards, to the direct decertification of a labor organization as is applied to elections to certify a representative, and for other purposes.

## § 1. National Mediation Board reform

Title I of the Railway Labor Act (45 U.S.C. 151 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> ## § 15. Decertification
>
> The Mediation Board shall apply the same procedures, including voting standards, to the direct decertification of a labor organization as is applied to elections to certify a representative.

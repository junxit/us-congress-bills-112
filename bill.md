# H.R. 5958

> To name the Jamaica Bay Wildlife Refuge Visitor Contact Station of the Jamaica Bay Wildlife Refuge unit of Gateway National Recreation Area in honor of James L. Buckley.

## § 1. Naming of Jamaica Bay Wildlife Refuge Visitor Contact Station, Jamaica Bay Wildlife Refuge unit, Gateway National Recreation Area

- **(a)** *Naming* The Jamaica Bay Wildlife Refuge Visitor Contact Station of the Jamaica Bay Wildlife Refuge unit of Gateway National Recreation Area in the State of New York shall be known and designated as the “James L. Buckley Visitor Contact Station”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in any statute, rule, regulation, Executive order, publication, map, paper, or other document of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the James L. Buckley Visitor Contact Station.

# H.R. 3503

> To amend the Sarbanes-Oxley Act of 2002 to make Public Company Accounting Oversight Board disciplinary proceedings open to the public.

## § 1. Public proceedings

Section 105 of the Sarbanes-Oxley Act of 2002 (15 U.S.C. 7215) is amended—

- **(1)** by amending subsection (c)(2) to read as follows:
  > - **(2)** *Public hearings* Hearings under this section shall be public unless the Board, on its own motion or after considering the motion of a party, orders otherwise.

- **(2)** in subsection (d)(1)(C), by striking “(once any stay on the imposition of such sanction has been lifted)”.

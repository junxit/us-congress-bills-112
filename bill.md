# H.R. 2815

> To revise the Federal charter for the Blue Star Mothers of America, Inc., to reflect a change in eligibility requirements for membership.

## § 1. Modification of eligibility requirements for membership in Blue Star Mothers of America, Inc

Section 30504 of title 36, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (1)—
  - **(A)** by striking the text preceding subparagraph (A) and inserting “she is a mother (meaning a woman who filled the role of birth mother, adoptive mother, stepmother, foster-mother, grandmother, or legal guardian) of a person who—”; and
  - **(B)** in subparagraph (B), by striking “in World War II or the Korean hostilities”; and
- **(2)** in paragraph (2), by inserting “or is a United States citizen living outside the United States” before the period at the end.

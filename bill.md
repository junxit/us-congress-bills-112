# H.J. RES. 112

> Disapproving the rule submitted by the Internal Revenue Service relating to the health insurance premium tax credit.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Internal Revenue Service relating to the health insurance premium tax credit (published at 77 Fed. Reg. 30377 (Wednesday, May 23, 2012)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.

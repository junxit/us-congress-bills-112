# H.R. 1200

> To provide for health care for every American and to control the cost and enhance the quality of the health care system.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “American Health Security Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Findings; sense of the House of Representatives

- **(a)** *Findings* Congress finds as follows:
  - **(1)** The number of uninsured Americans rose by more than 4,000,000 between 2008 and 2009 to a total of 51,000,000, or more than 1 of every 6 Americans.
  - **(2)** Such rise in the number of uninsured Americans was the largest single-year increase since 1987 and was the result of a continued decline in private health coverage, primarily in employer-sponsored insurance.
  - **(3)** Small businesses around the country cannot afford to reinvest in their companies and create new jobs because their health care bills are going up 10 or 15 percent every year.
  - **(4)** American businesses are at an economic disadvantage, because their health care costs are so much higher than in other countries. Notably, automobile manufacturers spend more on health care for an automobile than on steel.
- **(b)** *Sense of the House of Representatives concerning urgency of a Medicare-for-All type single payer health care system* It is the sense of the House of Representatives that the 112th Congress should enact a Medicare-for-All Single Payer Health Care System to make American companies more competitive and to stimulate job creation.
- **(c)** *Sense of the House of Representatives concerning the status of health care* It is the sense of the House of Representatives that the 112th Congress should recognize and proclaim that health care is a human right.

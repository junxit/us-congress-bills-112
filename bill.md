# H.R. 4420

> To suspend temporarily the duty on certain acrylic filament tow.

## § 1. Certain acrylic filament tow

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.40.21 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to certain acrylic filament tow) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “1.2%” and inserting “Free”; and
  - **(2)** by striking “12/31/2012” and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) apply with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.

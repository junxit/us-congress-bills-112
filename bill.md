# S. 743

That the bill from the Senate (S. 743) entitled “An Act to amend chapter 23 of title 5, United States Code, to clarify the disclosures of information protected from prohibited personnel practices; to require a statement in nondisclosure policies, forms, and agreements that such policies, forms, and agreements conform with certain disclosure protections; to provide certain authority for the Special Counsel; and for other purposes.”, do pass with the following

*Strike out all after the enacting clause and insert:*

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Whistleblower Protection Enhancement Act of 2012”.

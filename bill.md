# H.R. 5843

> To amend the Homeland Security Act of 2002 to permit use of certain grant funds for training conducted in conjunction with a national laboratory or research facility.

## § 1. Use of grant funds for projects conducted in conjunction with a national laboratory or research facility

Section 2008(a)(2) of the Homeland Security Act of 2002 (6 U.S.C. 609(a)(2)) is amended by inserting “training conducted in conjunction with a national laboratory or research facility and” after “including”.

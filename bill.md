# H.R. 4248

> To authorize the burial at Arlington National Cemetery of members of the Army who served honorably in the Tomb Guard Platoon of the 3d United States Infantry Regiment, which provides the sentinels at the Tomb of the Unknowns at Arlington National Cemetery.

## § 1. Burial of deceased former members of the Tomb Guard Platoon at Arlington National Cemetery

Section 4722(a) of title 10, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new paragraph:

> - **(3)** Subject to section 985(b) of this title, a person who served honorably as a member of the Army and was assigned to duty for at least nine months in the Tomb Guard Platoon of the 3d United States Infantry Regiment, which provides the sentinels at the Tomb of the Unknowns at Arlington National Cemetery, is eligible for interment or inurnment at Arlington National Cemetery.

# H.R. 6267

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to eliminate the tax on Olympic medals won by United States athletes.

## § 1. Elimination of tax on Olympic medals

- **(a)** *In general* Section 74 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by adding at the end the following new subsection:
  > - **(d)** *Exception for Olympic medals and prizes* Gross income shall not include the value of any prize or award won by the taxpayer in athletic competition in the Olympic Games.

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to prizes and awards received after December 31, 2011.

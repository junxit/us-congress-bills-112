# S. 997

> To authorize the Secretary of the Interior to extend a water contract between the United States and the East Bench Irrigation District.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “East Bench Irrigation District Water Contract Extension Act”.

## § 2. Authority to extend water contract

The Secretary of the Interior may extend the contract for water services between the United States and the East Bench Irrigation District, numbered 14–06–600–3593, until the earlier of—

- **(1)** the date that is 4 years after the date on which the contract would have expired if this Act had not been enacted; or
- **(2)** the date on which a new long-term contract is executed by the parties to the contract.

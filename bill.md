# H.R. 4614

> To extend the suspension of duty on Carfentrazone-ethyl and formulations thereof.

## § 1. Carfentrazone-ethyl and formulations thereof

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.01.54 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to Carfentrazone-ethyl and formulations thereof) is amended by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.

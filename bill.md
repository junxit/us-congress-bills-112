# H.R. 305

> To waive the time limitations specified by law for the award of certain military decorations in order to allow the posthumous award of the Medal of Honor to Doris Miller for actions while a member of the Navy during World War II.

## § 1. Waiver of time limitations on award of Medal of Honor to Doris Miller for valor as a member of the Navy during World War II

The time limitations specified in sections 6248(a) and 6250 of title 10, United States Code, shall not apply with respect to the posthumous award of the Medal of Honor under section 6241 of such title to Doris Miller (service number 356–12–35) for acts of heroism during World War II while a member of the United States Navy.

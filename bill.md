# H.J. RES. 94

> Making further continuing appropriations for fiscal year 2012, and for other purposes.

That the Continuing Appropriations Act, 2012 (Public Law 112–36) is further amended by striking the date specified in section 106(3) and inserting “December 17, 2011”.

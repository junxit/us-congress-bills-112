# H.R. 1384

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to temporarily increase the investment tax credit for geothermal energy property.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Geothermal Tax Parity Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Temporary increase in investment tax credit for geothermal energy property

- **(a)** *In general* Subclause (II) of section 48(a)(2)(A)(i) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “paragraph (3)(A)(i)” and inserting “clause (i) or (iii) of paragraph (3)(A)”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to property placed in service after the date of the enactment of this Act.

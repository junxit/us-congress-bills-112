# H.R. 4386

> To amend the Balanced Budget and Emergency Deficit Control Act of 1985 to eliminate the adjustment for disaster funding.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Budget for Disasters Act of 2012”.

## § 2. Elimination of adjustment for disaster funding

Section 251(b)(2) of the Balanced Budget and Emergency Deficit Control Act of 1985 is amended by striking subparagraph (D) (related to disaster funding).

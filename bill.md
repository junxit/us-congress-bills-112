# H.R. 1671

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to extend the authority of the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to provide specially adapted housing assistance to individuals residing temporarily in housing owned by a family member.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Andrew Connolly Veterans’ Housing Act”.

## § 2. Extension of authority of the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to provide specially adapted housing assistance to individuals residing temporarily in housing owned by a family member

Section 2102A of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “December 31, 2011” and inserting “December 31, 2016”.

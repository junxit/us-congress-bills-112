# H.J. RES. 104

> Disapproving a rule submitted by the Department of Labor relating to Temporary Non-agricultural Employment of H–2B Aliens in the United States.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of Labor relating to Temporary Non-agricultural Employment of H–2B Aliens in the United States (published in the Federal Register on February 21, 2012), and such rule shall have no force or effect.

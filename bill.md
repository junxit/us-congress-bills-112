# H.R. 445

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to make permanent the deduction of State and local general sales taxes.

## § 1. Permanent extension of deduction of state and local general sales taxes

Subparagraph (I) of section 164(b)(5) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “, and before January 1, 2012”.

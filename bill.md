# H.R. 3197

> To name the Department of Veterans Affairs medical center in Spokane, Washington, as the Mann-Grandstaff Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center.

## § 1. Name of Department of Veterans Affairs medical center, Spokane, Washington

The Department of Veterans Affairs medical center in Spokane, Washington, shall after the date of the enactment of this Act be known and designated as the “Mann-Grandstaff Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center”. Any reference to such medical center in any law, regulation, map, document, record, or other paper of the United States shall be considered to be a reference to the Mann-Grandstaff Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center.

# S. 1892

> To protect the housing rights of victims of domestic violence, dating violence, sexual assault, and stalking, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Housing Rights for Victims of Domestic and Sexual Violence Act of 2011”.

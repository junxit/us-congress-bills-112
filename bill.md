# H.R. 500

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to increase the manufacturer limitation on the number of new qualified plug-in electric drive motor vehicles eligible for credit.

## § 1. Increase in manufacturer limitation on the number of qualified plug-in electric drive motor vehicles eligible for credit

Paragraph (2) of section 30D(e) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “200,000” and inserting “500,000”.

# S. RES. 6

> To make effective appointment of Senate Legal Counsel.

That the appointment of Morgan J. Frankel of the District of Columbia to be Senate Legal Counsel, made by the President pro tempore this day, shall become effective as of January 7, 2011, and the term of service of the appointee shall expire at the end of the One Hundred Thirteenth Congress.

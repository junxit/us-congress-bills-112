# H.R. 3783

That the bill from the House of Representatives (H.R. 3783) entitled “An Act to provide for a comprehensive strategy to counter Iran’s growing hostile presence and activity in the Western Hemisphere, and for other purposes.”, do pass with the following

*On page 11, strike lines 17–19 and insert the following:*

- **(d)** *Form* The strategy in this section may be submitted in classified form, but shall include an unclassified summary of policy recommendations to address the growing Iranian threat in the Western Hemisphere.

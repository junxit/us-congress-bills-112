# H.R. 1235

> To provide a Federal regulatory moratorium, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Regulation Moratorium Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Federal regulatory moratorium

Except as provided in section 3, no department or agency of the United States shall put into force any rule until January 31, 2013.

## § 3. Exceptions to moratorium

Section 2 does not apply in the case of a rule excepted by section 553(a) from the requirements of that section or for which notice of proposed rulemaking is not required under subsection 553(b).

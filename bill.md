# H. RES. 351

> Expressing the sense of the House of Representatives that the United States Postal Service should issue a semipostal stamp to support medical research relating to Alzheimer’s disease.

That it is the sense of the House of Representatives that the United States Postal Service should, in accordance with section 416 of title 39, United States Code—

- **(1)** issue a semipostal stamp to support medical research relating to Alzheimer’s disease; and
- **(2)** transfer to the National Institutes of Health for that purpose any amounts becoming available from the sale of such stamp.

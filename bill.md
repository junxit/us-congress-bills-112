# H.R. 5821

> To provide for duty free treatment for certain United States Government property returned to the United States.

## § 1. Duty free treatment for certain United States Government property returned to the United States

- **(a)** *In general* Subchapter I of chapter 98 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended by inserting in numerical sequence the following new heading:
- **(b)** *Effective Date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.

# H.R. 3231

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to increase the amount allowed as a deduction for start-up expenditures.

## § 1. Increase in amount allowed as deduction for start-up expenditures

- **(a)** *In general* Paragraph (3) of section 195(b) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended to read as follows:
  > - **(3)** *Special rule for taxable years beginning in 2011, 2012, 2013, or 2014* In the case of a taxable year beginning in 2011, 2013, or 2014, paragraph (1)(A)(ii) shall be applied—
  >   - **(A)** by substituting “$20,000” for “$5,000”, and
  >   - **(B)** by substituting “$75,000” for “$50,000”.

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to amounts paid or incurred in taxable years beginning after December 31, 2010.

# H.R. 6193

> To extend the special immigrant religious professionals program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Religious Workers Visa Act”.

## § 2. Special immigrant religious professionals program

Section 101(a)(27)(C)(ii)(II) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1101(a)(27)(C)(ii)(II)) is amended by striking “2012,” and inserting “2017,”.

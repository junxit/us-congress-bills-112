# H.R. 6515

> To stop excessive toll hikes which harm our local economies, small businesses, and hard working families.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stop Excessive Toll Hikes Act”.

## § 2. Prohibition on excessive tolls

Section 5307 of title 49, United States Code, is amended in subsection (c)(1), after subparagraph (L), by inserting the following:

> - **(M)** Has not raised the maximum tolls for any bridge or tunnel by more than ten percent during the previous five fiscal years.

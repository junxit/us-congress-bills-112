# H.R. 2903

> To reauthorize the programs and activities of the Federal Emergency Management Agency.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “FEMA Reauthorization Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of urban search and rescue response system

Section 634 of Public Law 109–295 (6 U.S.C. 722) is amended by striking subsection (b) and inserting the following:

> - **(b)** *Authorization of appropriations* There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out the system $35,250,000 for each of fiscal years 2012 and 2013.

## § 3. Reauthorization of emergency management assistance compact grants

Section 661 of Public Law 109–295 (6 U.S.C. 761) is amended by striking subsection (d) and inserting the following:

> - **(d)** *Authorization of appropriations* There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this section $2,000,000 for each of the fiscal years 2012 and 2013. Such sums shall remain available until expended.

## § 4. Reauthorization of federal emergency management and administration

Section 699 of Public Law 109–295 (6 U.S.C. 811) is amended:

- **(1)** by striking “administration and operations” each place it appears and inserting “management and administration”;
- **(2)** in paragraph (2) by striking “and”;
- **(3)** in paragraph (3) by striking the period and inserting “; and”; and
- **(4)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(4)** for fiscal year 2012, $813,000,000, including amounts transferred from the Disaster Relief Fund; and
  > - **(5)** for fiscal year 2013, $813,000,000, including amounts transferred from the Disaster Relief Fund.

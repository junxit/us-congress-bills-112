# S. 976

> To extend the designation of Monroe County, Pennsylvania, as a HUBZone, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Monroe County HUBZone Act of 2011”.

## § 2. HUBZone designation

For purposes of the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 631 et seq.), Monroe County, Pennsylvania, shall be a HUBZone until not earlier than October 1, 2014.

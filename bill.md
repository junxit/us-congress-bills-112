# H.R. 6061

> To amend the Social Security Act to ensure the continuation of services under the Work Incentives Planning and Assistance program and the Protection and Advocacy for Beneficiaries of Social Security program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “WIPA and PABSS Continuation of Services Act of 2012”.

## § 2. Continuation of services under the Work Incentives Planning and Assistance program and the Protection and Advocacy for Beneficiaries of Social Security program

- **(a)** *Work Incentives Planning and Assistance program* Section 1149 of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1320b–20) is amended by striking subsection (e).
- **(b)** *Protection and Advocacy for Beneficiaries of Social Security program* Section 1150 of such Act (42 U.S.C. 1320b–21) is amended by striking subsection (h).

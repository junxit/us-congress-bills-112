# H.R. 3673

> To prohibit an increase in the compensation of Members of Congress from taking effect unless Congress consents to the increase by concurrent resolution.

## § 1. Prohibiting increase in Member salaries without consent of Congress

- **(a)** *Consent of Congress* Section 601(a)(2) of the Legislative Reorganization Act of 946 (2 U.S.C. 31(2)) is amended—
  - **(1)** in subparagraph (A), by striking “Subject to subparagraph (B)” and inserting “Subject to subparagraphs (B) and (C)”; and
  - **(2)** by adding at the end the following new subparagraph:
    > - **(C)** A percentage adjustment under subparagraph (A) may not take effect with respect to a calendar year unless, before January 1 of the year, Congress by concurrent resolution agrees that the percentage adjustment shall take effect. Each House shall vote on the adoption of such a concurrent resolution by a roll call vote.

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to 2012 and each succeeding year.

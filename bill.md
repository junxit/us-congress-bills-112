# H.R. 1192

> To extend the current royalty rate for soda ash.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Soda Ash Royalty Extension, Job Creation, and Export Enhancement Act of 2012”.

## § 2. Extension of royalty rate on soda ash

Section 102 of Public Law 109–338 (30 U.S.C. 262 note) is amended by striking “5-year” and inserting “10-year”.

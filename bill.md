# S. 1497

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to extend for 3 years reasonable cost contracts under Medicare.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Medicare Cost Contract Extension Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Three-year extension of Medicare reasonable cost contracts

Section 1876(h)(5)(C)(ii) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395mm(h)(5)(C)(ii)) is amended, in the matter preceding subclause (I), by striking “January 1, 2013” and inserting “January 1, 2016”.

# H.R. 1308

> To amend the Ronald Reagan Centennial Commission Act to extend the termination date for the Commission, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Ronald Reagan Centennial Commission Act Amendments

- **(a)** *Final report due date* Section 7(c) of the Ronald Reagan Centennial Commission Act (Public Law 111–25; 36 U.S.C. 101 note prec.) is amended by striking “April 30, 2011” and inserting “November 30, 2011”.
- **(b)** *Termination* Section 8 of the Ronald Reagan Centennial Commission Act (Public Law 111–25; 36 U.S.C. 101 note prec.) is amended by striking “May 30, 2011” and inserting “December 31, 2011”.

# H.R. 1387

> To amend the Emergency Economic Stabilization Act of 2008 to give the Special Inspector General oversight over the Small Business Lending Fund.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Small Business Lending Fund Accountability Act of 2011”.

## § 2. TARP Special Inspector General Oversight

Section 121(c)(1) of the Emergency Economic Stabilization Act of 2008 (12 U.S.C. 5231(c)(1)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “section 101, and” and inserting “section 101,”; and
- **(2)** by inserting “and activities under section 4103, 4104, or 4105 of the Small Business Jobs Act of 2010 (Public Law 111–240),” before “including”.

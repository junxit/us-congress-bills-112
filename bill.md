# H.R. 4620

> To amend title 40, United States Code, to add certain counties in the State of Mississippi to the region represented by the Appalachian Regional Commission.

## § 1. Appalachian Region Defined

Section 14102(a)(1)(E) of title 40, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by inserting “Grenada,” after “Clay,”; and
- **(2)** by inserting “Tallahatchie,” after “Prentiss,”.

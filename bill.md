# H.R. 1059

> To protect the safety of judges by extending the authority of the Judicial Conference to redact sensitive information contained in their financial disclosure reports, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Extension of redaction authority concerning sensitive security information

Section 105(b)(3) of the Ethics in Government Act of 1978 (5 U.S.C. App.) is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (A), by striking “Marshall” and inserting “Marshals”; and
- **(2)** by striking subparagraph (E).

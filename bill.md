# S. 786

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to modify the appointment and grade of the Chief of the Army Medical Specialist Corps.

## § 1. Appointment and grade of Chief of the Army Medical Specialist Corps

Section 3070(b) of title 10, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in the first sentence, by striking “captain” and inserting “lieutenant colonel”; and
- **(2)** by inserting after the first sentence the following new sentence: “An appointee who holds a lower regular grade shall be appointed in the regular grade of brigadier general.”.

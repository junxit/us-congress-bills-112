# S. 498

> To ensure objective, independent review of task and delivery orders.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Independent Task and Delivery Order Review Extension Act of 2011”.

## § 2. Extension of sunset date for certain protests of task and delivery order contracts

Section 4106(f)(3) of title 41, United States Code, is amended to read as follows:

> - **(3)** *Effective period* Paragraph 1(B) and paragraph (2) of this subsection shall not be in effect after September 30, 2016.

# H.R. 6587

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 225 Simi Village Drive in Simi Valley, California, as the Postal Inspector Terry Asbury Post Office Building.

## § 1. Postal Inspector Terry Asbury Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 225 Simi Village Drive in Simi Valley, California, shall be known and designated as the Postal Inspector Terry Asbury Post Office Building.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the Postal Inspector Terry Asbury Post Office Building.

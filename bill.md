# H.R. 2737

> To amend title 5, United States Code, to make stillborn children eligible for optional life insurance coverage.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Kaytlynn Noggle Federal Life Insurance Equity Act”.

## § 2. Inclusion of stillborn children for purposes of life insurance payments

Section 8701(d)(1)(B) of title 5, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “(other than a stillborn child)”; and
- **(2)** by inserting “stillborn child,” after “adopted child,”.

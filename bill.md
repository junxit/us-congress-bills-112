# H.R. 1952

> To amend title 23, United States Code, to modify the deadline for filing a claim seeking judicial review of a permit, license, or approval issued by a Federal agency for a highway or public transportation capital project, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Deadline for claims relating to highway and public transportation capital projects

- **(a)** *In general* Section 139(l) of title 23, United States Code, is amended by striking “180 days” in each of paragraphs (1) and (2) and inserting “90 days”.
- **(b)** *Applicability* The amendments made by subsection (a) shall apply to claims arising after the date of enactment of this Act.

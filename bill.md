# H.R. 434

> To prevent the Secretary of the Treasury from hiring new employees to enforce the individual health insurance mandate.

## § 1. Prohibition on hiring of new employees to enforce individual health insurance mandate

The Secretary of the Treasury shall not hire any employee to carry out or enforce section 5000A of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to requirement to maintain minimum essential coverage).

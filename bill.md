# S. 639

> To authorize to be appropriated $950,000,000 for each of the fiscal years 2012 through 2015 to carry out the State Criminal Alien Assistance Program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “SCAAP Reauthorization Act”.

## § 2. Extension of authorization of appropriations for the State Criminal Alien Assistance Program

Subparagraph (C) of section 241(i)(5) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1231(i)(5)) is amended by striking “2011.” and inserting “2015.”.

# H.R. 5141

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on certain 6-volt batteries.

## § 1. Certain 6-volt batteries

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.23.56 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to certain 6-volt batteries) is amended by striking “12/31/2012” and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) apply with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.

# S. 950

> To amend title 23, United States Code, to repeal a prohibition on allowing States to use toll revenues as State matching funds for Appalachian Development Highway projects.

## § 1. Matching funds for Appalachian Development Highway projects

Section 120(j)(1)(A) of title 23, United States Code, is amended by striking “and the Appalachian development highway system program under section 14501 of title 40”.

# S. 2636

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on mixtures of C5–18 perfluorocarbon alkanes, perfluorocarbon amines, and/or perfluorocarbon ethers.

## § 1. Mixtures of C5–18 perfluorocarbon alkanes, perfluorocarbon amines, and/or perfluorocarbon ethers

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.40.88 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to mixtures of C5–18 perfluorocarbon alkanes, perfluorocarbon amines, and/or perfluorocarbon ethers) is amended by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.

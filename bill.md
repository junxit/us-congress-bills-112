# H.R. 5591

> To modify and extend the temporary reduction of duty on β-cyfluthrin.

## § 1. β-cyfluthrin

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.02.64 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to β-cyfluthrin) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “4.8%” and inserting “2.1%”; and
  - **(2)** by striking “12/31/2012” and inserting “12/31/2015”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) apply with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.

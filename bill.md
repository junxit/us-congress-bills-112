# H.R. 2118

> To amend the National Labor Relations Act relating to the authority to enjoin State laws that are preempted by or conflict with such Act.

## § 1. Restriction on litigation authority

Section 10 of the National Labor Relations Act (29 U.S.C. 160) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(n)** The Board shall have no authority to enjoin or otherwise restrain the application or enforcement of any provision of a law of a State or political subdivision of a State on the grounds that such provision of law is preempted by or conflicts with any provision of this Act. The Board may refer its determination regarding such a law to the Attorney General who may institute an action described in the previous sentence under appropriate legal authority.

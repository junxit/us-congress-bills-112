# H.R. 1010

> To provide for expedited consideration by the Supreme Court of certain actions challenging the constitutionality of certain provisions of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act.

## § 1. Expedited review for certain actions

Notwithstanding section 2101(b) of title 28, United States Code, if any petition for writ of certiorari under Rule 11 of the Rules of the Supreme Court of the United States (adopted on January 12, 2010) is filed not later than the date of enactment of this Act to review an action brought to challenge the constitutionality of any provision of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act (Public Law 111–148) or any amendment made by such Act, it shall be the duty of the Supreme Court of the United States to advance on the docket and to expedite to the greatest possible extent the disposition of the action and appeal.

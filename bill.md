# S. 3446

> To amend the Endangered Species Act of 1973 to halt the premature proposed listing of 4 central Texas salamander species resulting from a settlement agreement, and to take into account extensive, ongoing State and local conservation efforts.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Salamander Community Conservation Act”.

## § 2. Certain salamanders

Section 10 of the Endangered Species Act of 1973 (16 U.S.C. 1539) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(k)** *Certain salamanders* Section 4(a) shall not apply to—
>   - **(1)** the Austin blind salamander;
>   - **(2)** the Georgetown salamander;
>   - **(3)** the Jollyville Plateau salamander; or
>   - **(4)** the Salado salamander.

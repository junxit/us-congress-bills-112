# H.R. 5143

> To prohibit the Secretary of Agriculture from making payments to the Brazilian Cotton Institute.

## § 1. Prohibition on payments to Brazilian Cotton Institute

The Secretary of Agriculture may not directly, or through the Commodity Credit Corporation, make payments to the Brazilian Cotton Institute.

# H.R. 4482

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to make permanent home loan guaranty programs for veterans regarding adjustable rate mortgages and hybrid adjustable rate mortgages.

## § 1. Making permanent certain home loan guaranty programs for veterans

- **(a)** *Extension of Demonstration Project on Adjustable Rate Mortgages* Section 3707(a) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “carry out a demonstration project under this section during fiscal years 1993 through 2012” and inserting “carry out a program”.
- **(b)** *Extension of Demonstration Project on Hybrid Adjustable Rate Mortgages* Section 3707A(a) of such title is amended by striking “carry out a demonstration project under this section during fiscal years 2004 through 2012” and inserting “carry out a program”.

# S. 1088

> To provide increased funding for the reinsurance for early retirees program.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Retiree Health Coverage Protection Act”.

## § 2. Increased funding for early retiree reinsurance

Section 1102(e) of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act (42 U.S.C. 18002(e)) is amended by striking “$5,000,000,000” and inserting “$10,000,000,000”.
